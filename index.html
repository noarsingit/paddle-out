Paddle Out

Warm water, palm trees, and the oruwa fishing boats are back on their moorings. The sets are rolling in, so grab your longboard and get out there before the wind turns.

  1. Paddle ↑ out to the peak buoy. Duck dive under the whitewater with Shift. Grab king coconuts on the way for extra paddle power.
  2. Wait at the peak until a wave comes.
  3. Turn around ↓ and paddle back toward the beach as the wave reaches you. A yellow circle fills up.
  4. When the wave reaches you, press Space: you stand up and take the wave.
  5. Ride all the way to the FINISH line near the beach. Cross it 3 times to win the session. Grab blue Ceylon sapphires on the wave for bonus points.
  6. Show off! While riding, press X to jump with the board (hold ← or → in the air to spin a 360) and Z to dance on the board.
  7. Ride the shoulder away from the curl. Stay in the pocket for double points; carve back for a cutback.
